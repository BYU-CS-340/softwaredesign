# Midterm 1 Exam
  
This exam will be taken in the testing center. It will be available starting at 8:00 am on Thursday, October 1st. It must be started by 9:00 pm on Tuesday, October 6th. The testing center will charge a late fee for any exam taken on October 6th.

There is no time limit for the exam, but you should give yourself two to three hours to complete the exam.

The exam covers the first third of the course, from the beginning of the semester through User Interface Architecture. See [MIDTERM 1 - Preparation Help](../midterm-1-review/midterm-1-review.md).
