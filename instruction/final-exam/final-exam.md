# Final Exam
  
This exam will be taken in the testing center. It will be available starting at 8:00 am on Saturday, December 12th. It must be started by 8:00 pm on Thursday, December 17th. The testing center does not charge late fees for final exams.

There is no time limit for the exam, but you should give yourself two to three hours to complete the exam.

The final exam is cumulative. It heavily emphasizes the topics taught after Midterm 2, but some questions do cover earlier material. See [FINAL EXAM - Preparation Help](../final-review/final-review.md).
